Need samples quickly?

We can often offer a quick turnaround on short production runs for brands needing samples for events, trade shows, buyer meetings, investor presentations or retailer discussions.

This allows you to present a finished, professional product without committing to a large production run too early. Timelines depend on the recipe, packaging, ingredients and testing requirements, but we are used to helping clients move quickly when there is a clear deadline.

Drinks we can help develop

We work across a wide range of soft drinks, functional drinks, mixers and RTDs.

  • Premium soft drinks, mixers and tonics
  • Non-alcoholic spirits and RTDs
  • Vodka-based and alcoholic RTDs
  • Iced teas, yerba mate and botanical drinks
  • Functional and adaptogenic beverages

Botanical and ingredient research

Alongside drink development, we can support more experimental botanical and ingredient work, including small-scale plant distillation, hydrosol production, botanical extraction trials and flavour exploration from herbs, flowers and plants.

This is particularly useful for brands looking to create something distinctive using natural, botanical or field-to-bottle ingredients.

Who we work with

We support start-ups, existing drinks brands, hospitality groups, agencies, wellness companies and businesses looking to launch or improve a beverage product.

Some clients come to us with a detailed brief and target specification. Others have a rough idea, a kitchen sample or a flavour they want to bring to life.

How projects usually work

Initial discussion

We review the product idea, target market, packaging format, production goals and budget.

Scope and proposal

We agree the support needed, such as recipe development, refinement, testing or short-run production.

Recipe and samples

We create or refine the drink, produce samples and make adjustments based on feedback.

Technical review

We consider pH, shelf life, allergens, nutrition, label information and production method.

Trial production

Where required, we produce a short run for market testing, launch stock or buyer samples.

Documentation

We can provide recipe sheets, technical data sheets, production notes and lab results where applicable.

Pricing

Every project is different, so pricing depends on the scope of work, complexity of the recipe, ingredients, packaging format, testing requirements and production volume.

Typical factors include the number of products or flavours, level of recipe development required, ingredient complexity, whether lab testing is needed, packaging format, production volume and the level of technical documentation required.

Once we understand the project, we can provide a clear proposal or quotation.
